# Login Customization

**Admin Settings > Customization** changes the public login screen. It does not change who may sign in or how accounts are authenticated.

## Design and Background

- **Login page design:** choose the layout shown in the preview.
- **Background Image:** upload or remove the image used by compatible designs.
- **Show background image:** displays the uploaded image when the selected design supports it.
- **Background image fit**, **Background image size (%)**, and **Background overlay opacity:** control framing, scale, and text readability.
- **Design background color:** supplies the design surface or fallback color.

Follow the upload limits shown in the UI. Test important subjects near the crop edges at narrow and wide window sizes.

## Branding

- **Show custom logo:** displays the logo managed under **Admin Settings > General**.
- **Custom logo height:** controls its displayed size.
- **Show branding message**, **Welcome headline**, and **Welcome message:** add optional guidance for visitors.

Use the preview as a starting point, then check the actual signed-out page on mobile and desktop, in light and dark appearance, at high zoom, and with the background hidden. Keep copy concise, maintain sufficient contrast, and do not publish private hostnames, credentials, or internal recovery instructions.
