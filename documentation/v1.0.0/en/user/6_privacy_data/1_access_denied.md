# Access Restrictions

Omlorix can restrict access because of your account status, your group's access hours, an approved-network requirement, too many sign-in attempts, required terms acceptance, or an organization policy. Read the complete message and any displayed return time before retrying.

## What to do

- Wait until the displayed return time or lockout countdown ends.
- Connect to the required organization network or VPN.
- Complete a displayed password-change, privacy-policy, or terms step when Omlorix requires it.
- If the account is inactive, pending, expired, or not allowed, contact your Omlorix support contact.
- For a temporary evaluation problem, wait briefly, reload once, and try again.

Repeated attempts do not bypass a schedule, network, or account restriction and can extend a lockout. Do not try to remove a group notice or watermark from a chat; it communicates a policy that applies to your work.

## Contact support safely

Provide the exact message, date and time, time zone, account email, Omlorix address, and a redacted screenshot. Mention whether other people on the same network are affected and whether the problem follows your account to another approved browser or network.

Never send a password, passkey, two-factor code, recovery code, API key, session information, or other secret. Use **Contact Support** when the page provides it.
