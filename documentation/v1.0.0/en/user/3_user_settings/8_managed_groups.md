# Managed Groups

**Managed Groups** appears only when you have a delegated **Owner**, **Manager**, or **Coordinator** role for at least one group. This is delegated group management, not full Omlorix administration. Select a group carefully when names are similar; the displayed path identifies its place in the hierarchy.

| Role | Settings | Rosters | Promote | Temporary accounts |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Owner** | Edit | View | Yes | Yes |
| **Manager** | Edit | View | No | Yes |
| **Coordinator** | View | View | No | Yes |

## Settings and members

Use **Settings** to manage the options delegated to you. These can include **Group context**, **Allow temporary chat**, file uploads and storage, temporary accounts, **Feature access**, and **Feature sharing** for projects, Todo, Notes, Memories, Skills, Prompts, Bookmarks, Agents, and Automations. A child group can be more restrictive than its parent but cannot enable access or sharing the parent blocks.

Select **Save Settings** after editing. Keep **Group context** appropriate for every member because it can be used in their chats.

**Managers** and **Members** show the group roster. Owners can use **Promote Member** to give an eligible direct member a higher delegated role. This page does not move or delete regular users or remove an existing manager.

## Temporary access

When allowed, **Temporary Access** creates expiring sign-ins for guests or workshops. Choose **Accounts to create** and **Valid for (hours)**, then copy the generated credentials immediately; they are shown once. You can review their status and **Revoke** an active temporary account.

Send credentials through a secure channel and revoke access when it is no longer needed. Expiration or revocation prevents future use but does not itself erase retained data.
