# Accounts & Switching

One browser profile can remember several signed-in accounts for the same Omlorix site. Each slot has its own active session, and each account keeps its own chats, files, permissions, and settings.

## Add and switch

Open the profile menu and select **Add account**. Complete sign-in, then confirm the active name, email, and picture.

To switch, open the profile menu and choose another remembered account.

## Remove or log out

If all available account slots are in use, select **Manage accounts**. **Replace** signs in a different account in that slot. **Remove** signs that account out of the browser. Neither action deletes the account or its data.

**Logout** ends the active account's current session without removing other remembered slots. **Settings > Security > Log Out All Devices** ends every current session for that account and is appropriate after suspected exposure.

## Shared-device safety

Use separate browser or operating-system profiles when several people share a device. The account switcher is for convenience, not isolation between people.

Do not add accounts on a public or untrusted computer. Always verify the active account's name and email before uploading, connecting a service, sharing, importing, or deleting information.
