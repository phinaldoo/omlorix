# Chat Personality

Personality gives future chat replies a preferred tone and interaction style across models that receive this setting.

Open **Settings > Chat > Customize Personality** and choose **None**, **Standard**, **Professional**, **Friendly**, **Honest**, **Quirky**, **Efficient**, **Cynical**, or **Custom**. For **Custom**, enter the treatment or tone you want.

Keep **Custom** instructions short, general, and free of secrets. Use a [Prompt](../10_workspace/13_prompts.md), [Skill](../10_workspace/8_skills.md), [Project](../10_workspace/2_projects.md), or [Agent](../10_workspace/9_agents.md) for task-specific workflows and reference material.

Personality is guidance. Your current request, safety requirements, an Agent, Skills, and other context can take priority. It does not provide access to tools, models, files, or connections.

Changes affect future responses and do not rewrite earlier messages.
