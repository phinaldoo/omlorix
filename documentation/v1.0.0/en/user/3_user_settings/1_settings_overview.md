# Settings Overview

Open the profile menu and select **Settings**. On a narrow screen, use **Open navigation** to change pages. `Shift`+`Cmd/Ctrl`+`,` also opens or closes Settings.

Settings are organized into:

- **Account:** [Profile](2_profile_settings.md) and [Security](3_security_privacy.md).
- **Preferences:** [General](5_general_preferences.md), [Appearance](6_theme_appearance.md), [Chat](7_chat_settings.md), [Memory](11_memory_settings.md), [Bring Your Own Key](12_bring_your_own_key.md), and [Help](15_keyboard_shortcuts.md).
- **Usage:** [Usage Limits](14_usage_limits.md), when a limit applies to your account.
- **Data:** [Data Control](../6_privacy_data/2_data_controls.md), [Shared Items](../6_privacy_data/3_shared_items.md), and [Managed Groups](8_managed_groups.md) when you manage a group.

Most switches and selectors save when you change them. **Personal Information** uses **Save Changes**; security, deletion, sharing, and import actions can require confirmation or a fresh sign-in check.

Your organization can hide a page or control, restrict its choices, or manage your profile and sign-in methods. If this guide mentions an option you cannot see, it is not available to your account. Reload Omlorix after a recent permission change before concluding that access is still missing.
