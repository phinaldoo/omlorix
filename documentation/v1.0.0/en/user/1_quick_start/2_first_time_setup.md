# First-Time Setup

Your first sign-in may show required notices and a **Welcome to Omlorix** card. Review them before starting a chat.

## Welcome and privacy review

The welcome card shows whether AI access to selected personal profile fields is on or off.

- Select **Review AI access** to open the relevant Security settings.
- Select **Start chatting** when you are ready to continue.

If a separate privacy-policy notice appears first, read and complete it. Your organization can require acknowledgement before Omlorix becomes available.

## Recommended settings check

Open **Settings** and review only the sections available to you:

1. **General** — confirm **Language**, **Country**, **Time Zone**, and optional **Location**.
2. **Profile** — check your name and optional **Profile Picture**.
3. **Appearance** — choose **Theme Mode**, **Your message color**, and **Font**.
4. **Security** — review personal-information access and set up a password, **Passkeys**, or two-factor authentication when offered.
5. **Chat** — choose which chat controls are shown and whether new chats should use **Always Use Temporary Chat**.

Some choices may be unavailable, required, or managed by your organization. Prefer the least personal-information access that still supports your work, and enter a city or region rather than a precise address for optional location details.

Most choices can be changed later. A password change can sign you out, and passkey or two-factor setup may require an additional device or authenticator app.

See [Passkeys](../3_user_settings/4_passkeys.md), [Chat Settings](../3_user_settings/7_chat_settings.md), and [Security](../3_user_settings/3_security_privacy.md).
