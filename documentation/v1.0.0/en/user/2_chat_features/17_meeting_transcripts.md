# Meeting Transcripts

**Add meeting** turns an audio or video recording into a Markdown transcript saved to the chat and Workspace Files. The action appears only when meeting transcription is available to your account.

## Add a recording

Open the attachment menu, select **Add meeting**, and choose:

- the upload option for an existing audio or video file;
- **Microphone** for a live recording; or
- **Screen audio** for audio shared through the browser.

For recording, keep the dialog and shared source open, stop when finished, and review the selected item. Screen audio works best when you share a browser tab and enable audio in the browser’s sharing dialog.

Before **Transcribe meeting** becomes available, complete **Recording requirements**: confirm that participants were informed and any required consent was obtained, choose the legal basis, enter its details, and set the retention window.

## Review and retain responsibly

Omlorix extracts audio from video when necessary, creates a dated Markdown transcript file, and attaches it to the chat or current project. The **Add meeting** workflow does not save the source recording as a Workspace file; retain your authorized original separately if you need it. The transcript and its consent, legal-basis, and retention metadata do persist. Omlorix does not automatically summarize the meeting; ask in a separate message for a summary or action list.

On supported devices, Omlorix requests that the screen stay awake during transcription. The request can end when the page is hidden, the device is locked, or battery-saving rules take over, so do not rely on it to keep a capture running.

Check names, dates, specialist terms, speakers, decisions, and omissions against the recording. The selected retention window is recorded with the transcript, but you remain responsible for confirming that deletion follows your organization’s policy when the window ends.

Only transcribe recordings you are authorized to process. The media is sent to the configured transcription service, so remove unrelated or sensitive material first.
