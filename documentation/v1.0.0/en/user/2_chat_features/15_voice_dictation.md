# Voice Dictation

Voice Dictation inserts speech as editable text at the cursor in the message box. It never sends the message automatically and is available only when transcription and microphone access are enabled.

Place the cursor where the text belongs, select the microphone, allow access, and speak clearly. Select the control again to stop. During live transcription, the current dictated section updates in place; cancel it if you do not want to keep the transcription.

Review the text before sending. Correct names, numbers, dates, punctuation, specialist terms, and anything affected by background noise.

Dictation is unavailable during a Realtime Call. If the microphone is hidden or unavailable, check the browser's site permission, close another active transcription, and ask your support contact whether transcription is available to your account.

Audio is processed by the transcription service configured for your account. Share only information you are authorized to send. Dictation has its own usage allowance and can stop when the available minutes are reached.

See [Realtime Call](18_realtime_call.md), [Chat Settings](../3_user_settings/7_chat_settings.md), and [Usage Limits](../3_user_settings/14_usage_limits.md).
