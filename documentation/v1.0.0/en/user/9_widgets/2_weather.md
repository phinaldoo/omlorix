# Weather Widget

A weather request can display current conditions and a daily forecast when the Weather tool and selected model are available.

Ask for an unambiguous city and region. Confirm the resolved place, units, and forecast dates before relying on the result.

The card can show current temperature and conditions, daily high and low, humidity, wind, and future-day forecasts. Available details and number of days vary by location and data source.

Weather data can be delayed, incomplete, or wrong, and the displayed day follows the resolved location and data source. The widget is not an official warning service. Use authoritative local sources for severe weather, aviation, marine, wildfire, flood, or other safety-critical decisions.

Avoid entering a precise home, care site, shelter, customer location, or other sensitive address. The place can be sent to an external weather service.

Ask for a text summary with full dates, units, and the resolved place when you need an accessible or copyable alternative.
